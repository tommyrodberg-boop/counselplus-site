Questions

The fine print, in plain English.

Can't find it? Ask our assistant above.

Is this actually insurance, or a discount club?

It's insurance. When a covered matter goes to court, we pay the legal bills up to your plan's limit. Many "legal plans" only give you a discount on hourly rates. Ours pays.

Who are the lawyers?

Licensed attorneys at our independent panel law firm. They review and sign anything that counts as legal advice, and they represent you if your case goes to court. Our AI drafts documents to save time; it never gives you legal advice on its own.

Can I sign up when I already have a problem?

Yes, for advice: you can ask questions and get letters drafted from day one. Court coverage has a 90-day waiting period and doesn't apply to disputes that started before you joined. It's like any insurance: get it before you need it.

What isn't covered?

Criminal matters beyond traffic, divorce and custody litigation, business disputes on a personal plan, and anything that started before your coverage. Every plan still gets attorney advice on these, so you're never left guessing.

How does the deductible work?

On Essentials and Core, you pay the first $250 of each covered court matter. Advice, letters and documents never have a deductible. Premier has no deductible at all.

¿Puedo usarlo en español?

Sí. The app, the assistant and our attorneys all work in Spanish, from your first question to your day in court.
